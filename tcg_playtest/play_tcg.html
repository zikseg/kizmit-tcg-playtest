Quick rules & prototype notes
  • Each turn: draw, score 1 point per land holding your token, then take 2 actions.
  • Actions: move (click a dot), gather ink (2 on your own ink, else 1), rest to draw a card, claim a flagged land you stand on, clear an enemy token you stand on, or cast a sorcery (click a highlighted card).
  • Rough terrain (hatched) ends a move. Starting on your own ink gives +1 square.
  • Casting a spell reveals part of your sheet; the opponent's sheet fills in as they cast.
  • Win: knockout, or 15 holding points. Both decks empty → most points wins.
  • Prototype limits: the board is built automatically; your reactions (dashed cards) fire automatically when triggered; the computer peeks at your hand when judging danger.
